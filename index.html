💡 Proven Exam Tips (from those who passed)

1

Read the Exam Guide First

Before watching a single video, read the official AWS Exam Guide cover to cover. It's your blueprint — understand the 4 domains and their weights.

2

Watch at 1.25x + Handwritten Notes

Speed up the course but write notes by hand — it forces deeper processing. Making your own slides also helps retention enormously.

3

Practice Exams = Secret Sauce

Aim for 78-86% on practice exams before the real test. The actual exam is often easier than quality practice tests.

4

Use Gemini Pro as Your Tutor

Ask Gemini to explain complex topics, generate quiz questions, compare "SQS vs SNS vs Kinesis", and create scenario-based questions.

5

Hands-On > Theory Alone

A balanced approach is best. Complete labs for every major service using AWS Academy, Free Tier, or Skill Builder sandbox.

6

Look for Keywords in Questions

"Exactly once delivery" → SQS FIFO. "Least operational overhead" → managed/serverless. "Most cost-effective" → spot/reserved.

7

Eliminate Wrong Answers First

Most wrong options are blatantly complex or don't make sense. Narrow to 2, then use keywords to decide. Never leave blanks.

8

Flag & Review Strategy

First pass: answer known, flag uncertain ones. Second pass: revisit with fresh eyes. Aim to finish with 8+ minutes spare.

9

Focus on Security (30%)

Domain 1 carries the highest weight. Master IAM, VPC security, KMS, encryption, and Organizations — worth almost a third of the exam.

10

Use Cheat Sheets for Final Review

DigitalCloud and Cloudoku cheat sheets are perfect for quick revision the night before. Supplement, don't replace, deep study.

🚀 11-Day Accelerated Study Approach

Time is short! With the exam on June 29, you need to compress the 19-day plan into 11 days (about 1.7x speed). Here is the recommended approach:

  • The 1.7x Schedule (Days 1-7): Study 8-10 hours daily. Watch Stephane Maarek's course at 1.7x-2x speed. Focus heavily on Security (30%) and Resilient Architectures (26%).
  • Strategic Hands-On Labs: Only do labs for core compute and networking (VPC, EC2, IAM, ALB). Skip labs for simpler services.
  • Practice Exams Early (Days 8-10): Start mock exams with at least 3 days left. Do not just memorize answers—review explanations for wrong options.
  • AI Tutor: Use Gemini Pro to directly compare confusing services (e.g., SQS vs SNS) instead of re-watching long videos.
  • Final Review (Day 11): Learn no new material. Use cheat sheets for rapid-fire review and rest well!
